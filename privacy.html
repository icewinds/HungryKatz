Privacy policy

HungryKatz · last updated 6 October 2026

HungryKatz is a cosy cat-café game made for our family and shared with friends. It does not collect any personal information about you or your child.

What we collect

Nothing. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no adverts, no analytics or tracking, and no in-app purchases. The game never sends anything you do to us or to anyone else.

Your saved game

Your café (coins, levels, cats, outfits, stickers, high scores and the names typed into them, and your settings) is saved only on your own device, in the browser's local storage. It never leaves the device. Clearing the browser or app data deletes it.

The optional backup code in Settings is a copy of that save as text. It is only shown to you; it goes wherever you choose to paste it, and nowhere else.

Things the game downloads

The game, its fonts and pictures all come from this one website (hungrykatz.solutioncloud.tech, hosted on GitHub Pages). Like any website, the hosting service may keep standard technical server logs (such as IP address and time of request) for security and running the service. We do not use or receive these logs. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

HungryKatz is suitable for children. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Sharing

The Share buttons only open your device's own share menu (or copy a link). The game does not see who you share with.

Changes and contact

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions? Open an issue on the HungryKatz project page.
