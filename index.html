How to play

  1. 👆
    Tap the floor to walk your chef cat around the café. Tap several places and your cat visits each one in order.
  2. 🥛
    Step on a food pad by the kitchen to pick up food. You can carry some of every kind at once.
  3. 🐱
    Walk into a hungry cat that wants the food you're carrying. The ring above its head shows how long it will wait.
  4. 🐾
    Don't keep them waiting! A cat that waits too long leaves sad. Miss 10 cats and the game is over.
More tips

Earn more

  • ⬆️Spend coins on upgrades (up to level 10): carry more, walk faster, make cats wait longer, earn more tips and coins, and reach further.
  • 🔥Serve cats quickly one after another for a combo bonus.
  • 👑VIP cats wear crowns: they're impatient but pay triple!
  • 💰Happy cats sometimes leave a tip.
  • 🍽️On weekends some cats order a two-dish special.
  • 🎁Come back every day for a daily bonus.

Your café

  • 🏠Your café starts shabby. Level up to fix it up, add more tables and welcome more cats.
  • 🍣Level up your café to unlock new foods: cat food, fish, sushi and cupcakes.
  • 🐶Adopt café pets in Upgrades for handy bonuses.
  • ⚙️Pick a difficulty and a scene in Settings.
  • 🌸Little chefs can pick Relaxed in Settings: nobody leaves sad.

Collect

  • 🏅Collect stickers and buy hats and aprons in Choose cat.
  • 😺Buy new chef cats, or unlock the rarest ones with a high score.
