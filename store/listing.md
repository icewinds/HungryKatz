## Full description (max 4000)

Welcome to HungryKatz, the cosiest little café in town, where every customer is a hungry cat!

You are the waiter. Cats stroll in through the front door, ring the bell and sit down at the window bar and the tables. Chef Biscuit gets cooking: milk from the fridge, cupcakes from the oven and everything else sizzling on the stoves. When the plates land on the counter, grab them on your silver tray and serve each cat what it ordered before it runs out of patience.

WHAT'S ON THE MENU
• Tap to walk, or tap several spots and your cat visits each one in turn
• Serve milk, cat food, fish, sushi and cupcakes as your café levels up
• Watch your shabby little café grow into a fancy one, with more tables, a rug, flowers and candles
• Combos for quick service, VIP cats in crowns and two-dish weekend specials
• Upgrades: carry more, walk faster, cook faster, earn bigger tips
• Café pets: a goldfish, a parrot and a sleepy puppy
• Collect 15 stickers and buy hats and aprons for your waiter
• Unlock new cats, plus a secret one if you can find it
• Four café scenes, seasonal decorations, and a gnome who screams. Really.

MADE FOR KIDS AND GROWN-UPS
• Relaxed mode: nobody leaves sad and there is no game over, perfect for little ones
• Easy, Normal and Hard for everyone else
• Daily bonus for coming back

KIND TO YOU
• No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts
• Collects no personal data at all; your café is saved on your own phone
• Works offline once installed
• Back up your café with a code in Settings to move it to a new phone

HungryKatz was made with love for a little girl, and shared so everyone can enjoy it. Have fun, and keep those katz fed!
